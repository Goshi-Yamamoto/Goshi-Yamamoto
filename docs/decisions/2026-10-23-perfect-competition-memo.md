# Perfect Competition — Decision Memo
**To:** Farm Owner  
**Subject:** Recommended Crop Plan for the Upcoming Season

## Recommendation
I recommend planting **10 tomato beds, 20 carrot beds, and 30 mesclun beds**, for a total of **60 beds**. This plan is expected to generate about **$42,775 in net seasonal profit** after variable costs and the **$20,000 fixed cost**. It uses **60 of 64 beds** and about **5,277 of 6,480 labor hours**.

## Why This Plan
The main decision rule is **P >= MC**. For tomatoes, Bed 10 has a marginal cost of **$8,248.11**, which is below the market price of **$8,800**. However, Bed 11 has a marginal cost of **$9,390.17**, which is above the market price. Therefore, tomato production should stop at 10 beds.
For carrots and mesclun, the market price remains higher than marginal cost up to their maximum limits. Therefore, the farm should produce **20 carrot beds** and **30 mesclun beds**. This crop mix also leaves some unused land and about **1,200 labor hours** as a safety buffer.

## What Would Change My Recommendation
The recommendation could change if crop capacity limits, market prices, or labor costs change. For example, if the tomato price rises above **$9,390.17**, adding the 11th tomato bed may become profitable. If market price falls below **AVC**, the farm should consider stopping production of that crop in the short run.
